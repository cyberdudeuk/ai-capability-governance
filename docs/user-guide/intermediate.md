# Intermediate guide

For you if you use, buy or approve AI in your work and need to show it is governed.

## Routes by role

| You are | Start with | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Accountable owner or SRO | [H0–H5 review model](https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/ai-capability-governance/#assurance) | [Transparency record](https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/ai-capability-governance/#tool-atrs), [roadmap](https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/ai-capability-governance/#roadmap) |
| Supplier or delivery partner | [Resources, filtered by procurement and security](https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/ai-capability-governance/#resources) | [Catalogue metadata](https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/ai-capability-governance/#catalogue), [assurance](https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/ai-capability-governance/#assurance) |
| Data or AI governance lead | [Signals](https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/ai-capability-governance/#signals) | [Agent registry (TK-03)](https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/ai-capability-governance/#toolkit), [AI asset metadata](https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/ai-capability-governance/#catalogue) |

## Using the signals

[Signals](https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/ai-capability-governance/#signals) is a weekly horizon scan of AI and data governance. Each item names its author, links to where it
was found and says how far it has been checked. Summaries come from search-indexed text, so read the source before you
quote anything. Items marked *commercial interest* come from vendors, consultancies or law firms.

## Judging a source

The **[authority tiers](https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/ai-capability-governance/#knowledge)** say which source wins when two disagree: the lower tier number wins.
