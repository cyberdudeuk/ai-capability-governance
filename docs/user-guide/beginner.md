# Beginner guide

For you if AI in public services is new to you, or you are using this site for the first time. No technical knowledge needed.

## Start here

1. Read the **four principles** at the top of [the site](https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/ai-capability-governance/). They are the rules everything else follows.
2. Use the **[human review triage](https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/ai-capability-governance/#tool-triage)** (2 minutes). Answer six questions about your piece of work and get
   a review level from H0 (no review) to H5 (board approval), with what to do next.
3. If your work needs a record, use the **[evidence pack generator](https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/ai-capability-governance/#tool-evidence)** (3 minutes).

## Words you will meet

The **[glossary](https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/ai-capability-governance/#glossary)** explains 22 terms in plain English, including *agent*, *human in the loop* and *ATRS*
(the Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard).

## If AI is being used on your case

Read **[transparency and redress](https://cyberdudeuk.github.io/ai-capability-governance/#transparency)**: how to find out how AI was used, and how to challenge a decision.

## Ask for help

Use the [contact form](https://github.com/cyberdudeuk/ai-capability-governance/issues/new?template=contact.yml). It is public, so do not include personal details.
